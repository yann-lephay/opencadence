Three-panel photographic exercise storyboard for an ALTERNATING DEAD BUG. Wide image with three equal square panels side by side. A natural adult man wearing a dark forest-green T-shirt and shorts, barefoot on a dark green mat in a warm cream home with wood floor and a small orange accent. Subtle vintage printed-photo aesthetic. Fixed elevated three-quarter view showing the whole body with room above the head and beyond toes. Head rests on mat throughout. No text.
LEFT PANEL: neutral dead bug, both arms pointing to the ceiling, both hips and knees bent at right angles, shins parallel to floor.
CENTER PANEL: RIGHT ARM lowers overhead beside the ear while LEFT LEG extends straight, hovering above floor. Other arm remains vertical, other knee stays bent.
RIGHT PANEL: LEFT ARM lowers overhead beside the ear while RIGHT LEG extends straight hovering above floor. Other arm remains vertical, other knee stays bent.
The last two panels MUST show opposite diagonals, NOT the same pose repeated. In panel2 the camera-near leg is long and camera-far knee is bent; in panel3 camera-near knee is bent and camera-far leg is long. Keep all four limbs visible, natural anatomy, shoulders/back/pelvis on mat. Same camera, scene and body size. No mirrored scene, no crop, no duplicated limbs.

La Bonne Séance builds today’s strength workout from the time you have, the equipment actually available and the work you confirm.

BEFORE YOUR WORKOUT
• Choose 20, 30 or 45 minutes.
• Declare your exact equipment, supports and usable space.
• Change only today’s exception without rebuilding a rigid week.

DURING YOUR WORKOUT
• Keep the movement, reps, load and useful rest easy to read.
• Confirm only the work you actually complete.
• If you report discomfort, stop the movement or workout instead of trying to work around a health issue.

AFTER YOUR WORKOUT
• Workout history stays on your iPhone by default.
• Confirmed sets become useful starting points for the next workout.
• A shortened or missed workout creates no debt and no punitive catch-up.
